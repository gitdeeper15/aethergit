# Methodological Scope — AetherGit

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Status:** Deferred

---

## 1. Purpose

This document records the deferral of AetherGit's empirical methodological scope. It does not specify a methodology; it specifies why methodology is not specified in advance.

---

## 2. The Deferral

AetherGit is a **formal research paper**. It specifies three mathematical definitions, four architectural pillars, and two performance theorems. It does not execute empirical benchmarks.

Whether AetherGit should be empirically evaluated — and how — is deferred until the formal framework is complete.
